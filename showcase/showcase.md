# Find your angle.

Carom is a paddle duel with a twist in every return. Settle into the opening serve, track the ball across the dark court, and meet it with your mint-green paddle. The next shot is yours to shape: a clean center hit goes straight across, while an edge catch sends it racing toward a wall.

Move through the moment of contact and the ball carries your motion with it. Spin bends a safe-looking return into a late surprise, leaving a pale curved trail across the court. A quick slide can turn defense into a shot your opponent has to chase.

The two amber obstacles never quite sit still. They sway, rotate, and send bank shots off in new directions. Read the face before the bounce, find a gap, or use the obstacle as an accomplice. Each paddle hit adds speed, so a long rally becomes a test of both anticipation and nerve.

Take on the reactive computer in Solo or challenge a friend to a shared-keyboard Versus match. Race to eleven, then earn a two-point lead to finish it. At ten-all, there is no shortcut: just the next serve, the next ricochet, and one more chance to find your angle.
